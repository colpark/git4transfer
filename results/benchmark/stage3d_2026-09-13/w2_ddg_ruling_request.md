# W2 physics substitution: ruling requested

**13 September 2026 — for the panel; W2 remains closed.** This is a methods ruling, not a W2 result. The panel-approved W2 denominator is **238** sequence clusters with a usable non-WT MegaScale stability row. Those labels are assay-derived `ddG_ML` estimates from cDNA-display proteolysis; they are not generated by either physics tool ([Stage 1 finding](../stage1_2026-09-12/finding.md), [Tsuboyama et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06328-6)).

**What the intended Rosetta tool would measure.** A specified, reproducible Rosetta monomer-ΔΔG protocol would predict a *mutation-induced stability change* by modeling the WT and mutant in the same score function, repacking/minimizing both, and comparing their optimized scores. The [Rosetta `ddg_monomer` manual](https://docs.rosettacommons.org/docs/latest/application_documentation/analysis/ddg-monomer) recommends repeated WT/mutant models and a top-score summary; its sign convention is mutant minus WT, with negative predicting stabilization. This is an empirical **stability proxy in Rosetta energy units**, not a direct experimental folding free energy. A precise protocol, input structure, score function, and sign/scale convention would still need freezing before W2 scoring. Our `pyrosetta_ddg` tool has produced **no value**: the institutional license and executable aarch64 build remain unverified ([Stage 2 block](../stage2_2026-09-12/blocked.md), [certification](../stage2_2026-09-12/certification.csv)).

**What the installed OpenMM tool measures.** `openmm_delta_energy` subtracts the instantaneous potential energies of two user-supplied, prepared PDB conformations, each evaluated once on CPU with Amber14, GBN2 implicit solvent when water is absent (TIP3P when present), and `NoCutoff`. It performs **no mutation modeling, minimization, conformational sampling, unfolded-state calculation, or free-energy estimation** ([implementation](../../../benchmark-mcp/physics.py), [OpenMM `State.getPotentialEnergy` API](https://docs.openmm.org/8.0.0/api-c%2B%2B/generated/State.html)). Its equal-atom-count check also rejects many ordinary substitutions with different side-chain atom counts. The Stage 2 known-input result—identical prepared PDB on both sides gives 0 kJ/mol—and invalid-PDB control establish execution and failure handling, **not** ability to rank MegaScale stability effects ([Stage 2 block](../stage2_2026-09-12/blocked.md)). The two outputs cannot be equated by unit conversion or by renaming this number `ddG`.

**Does the W2 signal survive?** The **measured W2 ground truth survives** unchanged, and Arm 2 still has a genuine classical retrieve→score→compare→rank→filter→submit path ([Stage 2 finding](../stage2_2026-09-12/finding.md)). The proposed **Rosetta-like physics signal does not survive this swap**; neither its coverage nor its correlation with assay `ddG_ML` has been established. Treating OpenMM's snapshot-energy delta as a stability score would confound the arm comparison and can silently select only equal-atom-count variants. The smoke run's independent 76-aa ubiquitin structure control also did not match its 72-aa assay construct, so it is not a W2 mutation-effect validation.

**Requested ruling.** Choose and record one route before any W2 item or arm run: **(A)** retain W2 as an assay-label ranking workflow but expose OpenMM only as a clearly named structural diagnostic, never as `ddG` or a required stability ranker, and freeze identical tool grants/scoring conventions across arms; **(B)** obtain a licensed, executable Rosetta implementation and certify a mutation-specific protocol with positive and negative controls before restoring the physics claim; or **(C)** defer W2 if Rosetta-style physics is essential to the intended comparison. Our recommendation is **A** for the W2 assay comparison, with the physics claim explicitly removed; B is needed to test Rosetta ΔΔG itself. No W2 run is authorized by this document.
