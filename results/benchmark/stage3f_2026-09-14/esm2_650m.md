The served model is now `facebook/esm2_t33_650M_UR50D`, pinned revision `08e4846e537177426273712802403f7ba8261b6c`, CPU, float32. The MCP receipt/cache key includes model and revision; this prevents an identically parameterized request from returning an 8M cache hit. The worker still computes one-site **masked-marginal** natural-log odds on the full supplied ProteinGym `target_seq`, rejecting sequences above 1,022 residues rather than silently windowing.

Only the allowlisted mutant and released model-score columns were read; no DMS labels were opened. The score and rank agreement are a convention/weights check, not a DMS accuracy result.
