# Stage 3f finding — checkpoint repaired; single cell did not submit

**Panel questions answered, 14 September 2026 UTC.** ESM-2 was upgraded from the accidental 8M smoke checkpoint to pinned 650M (`08e4846e537177426273712802403f7ba8261b6c`, CPU float32). The MCP known-input, deliberately unmasked negative, invalid-input and receipt controls all passed. The 20-candidate label-blind comparison with released `ESM2_650M` gives **Spearman ρ = 1.0000**, maximum absolute score difference 1.36×10⁻⁵. The 1,644 old 8M calls are archived, not reused. Track A restarted from zero on the same 148 items/14,668 candidates and was resumed after the pilot; the new progress is separate and remains blind. ESM-IF1 is the named 142M release, identified locally by SHA-256 because its upstream loader exposes no revision; ESMFold is pinned v1 with the 3B LM backbone. Neither is an 8M smoke stand-in.

Node 11's hwmon critical limits belong to NVMe and network ASIC devices, **not** CPU/SoC. `acpitz` exposes no readable trip, `tegrastats` and `sensors` are absent. No CPU/SoC safety number was invented. The named measurement failure is throughput throttling: a fixed-prompt, three-repeat unloaded baseline was **48.15 tokens/s** and the declared 70% validity threshold **33.70 tokens/s** for a 60-second window. Must-fail and healthy synthetic controls passed. Temperatures, power, memory and utilization are reported only.

The one authorized pilot cell (item 2, Arm 3, guided, sample 1) took **80.45 s** and **did not submit**. It made 16 classical calls (7 valid, 9 invalid arguments), no calls to the other mounted servers, and ended with an ordinary agent message. This is a behavioural/answer-contract failure, not a thermal abort or a scored result. Generated output was capped at 1,200 tokens **per response**; 16 responses totaled 2,954 tokens. No answer means fabricated-call-ID rate, `rejected` population and evaluation depth are **unknown**, not zero. Peak GPU/CPU temperatures were 66.0/81.1 °C; model generation remained above threshold in every observed response, though no individual response lasted 60 seconds, so the sustained gate was not directly exercised.

**Recommendation:** Panel should price Stage 4 from this 80.45-second cell but not infer accuracy or proceed to the remaining 239 cells without ruling on the no-submission behaviour and whether a per-response 1,200-token cap is the intended harness limit. Stage 4 remains closed. No W2 run, cohort selection, scoring or label evaluation was performed.
