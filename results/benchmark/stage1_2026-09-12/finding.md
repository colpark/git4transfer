# Stage 1 finding — scarce-unit audit (12 September 2026)

**Panel recommendation, not a gate ruling:** W1 and W2 have enough sequence-separated units for the ≥30 band, subject to contamination controls. W3 and W4 are necessarily below 30 on the available evidence. W5 has ≥43 *nominal* distinguishable lanthanide-pair contrasts, but only eight measured protein constructs across nine assay conditions; shared ion measurements make the 43 contrasts correlated. The panel should decide whether that is independent enough for the planned analysis. No cohort, task specs, servers, model calls, or GPU work were made.

**W1.** ProteinGym substitution reference at tag **PG_v1.3** (tag dated 28 April 2025); assay papers date 2010–2023. Assay size in total substitutions: min 63, quartiles 1,332 / 2,339 / 6,769, max 536,962; total 2,465,767. There are 56 organisms; largest are *H. sapiens* 96, *E. coli* 17, *S. cerevisiae* 14, *M. musculus* 9. Taxon counts: human 96, prokaryote 50, other eukaryote 40, virus 31. Year frequencies: 2010:1, 2012:4, 2013:4, 2014:7, 2015:9, 2016:17, 2017:10, 2018:12, 2019:17, 2020:17, 2021:10, 2022:22, 2023:87. All 217 assays / 174 clusters predate a 2025 *publication* cutoff; zero postdate it. The loss from 217 to 174 is 19.8% of raw units, just below the specified >20% reduction rule, although raw count overstates effective n by 24.7% relative to clustered n. Use 174, never 217, for independent intervals. Publication age is exposure, not proof of memorization. [ProteinGym source](https://github.com/OATML-Markslab/ProteinGym/tree/PG_v1.3).

**W2.** Tsuboyama MegaScale has 331 natural and 148 designed domain names (479); 50%-identity clustering yields 242 clusters (142 natural-only, 98 designed-only, two mixed). Raw count falls 49.5%, so it is misleading by the stated rule. There are 412 domains / 238 clusters with at least one usable non-WT `ddG_ML` row. The paper was published 19 July 2023; the [Zenodo dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/7992926) is a 2023 release (metadata publication date 21 April; record created 1 June). All 242 clusters predate 2025. One domain lacking a WT row was reconstructed from its released PDB sequence solely for clustering, with provenance in `w2_domains.csv`. [Nature paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06328-6).

**W3–W4.** Replication cell is BioDesignBench mini-protein-binder × de novo: 19 tasks, no binder-redesign cell. Even if every task has a different target, n≤19; the private specifications, true target identities, and pre-2025 fraction are not publicly recoverable. The public trypsin-binder demo is explicitly outside the 76 private tasks. [Paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13174699/), [repository](https://github.com/RomeroLab/BioDesignBench). W4 has two *named* validated archetypes in the [implementation plan](https://github.com/colpark/git4transfer/blob/main/19C_benchmark_implementation_plan.md); coordination number/donor geometry was not independently measured. Its four asset totals are plan assertions, not validated family counts.

**W5.** An RCSB sequence/API audit of the local 822-chain REE source found 327 cross-deposit PDB-entry pairs covering eight *exact* protein sequences and five Ca/REE element pair types. Calcium and REE in separate deposits show occupancy, not preferential binding, and Ca need not contact the matched polymer; these are permissive structural candidates, **zero usable selectivity labels**. Four 3-aa source rows could not enter the API's ≥10-aa search. A conservative within-protein/condition audit of published full-length-protein `Kd,app` tables found 85 unique lanthanide pairs measured and 43 with non-overlapping mean ±2 published SD; no free-peptide or cross-pH comparison was counted. This is a screening rule, not a formal significance test. The 43 derive from eight constructs in nine conditions, predominantly related lanmodulins; the effective uncertainty cannot be estimated as 43 independent assays. Sources: [CaM-LBT study](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejic.202500468), [LanM family study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13128445/), [heavy-LanM study and SI](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c08525.s001), [RCSB APIs](https://search.rcsb.org/).

**Ground truth and tools.** The plan's 17/93 measured-task claim and five RESCUE task labels cannot be resolved individually: no merged manifest, task IDs, or assay provenance are in the public transfer. Local REE positive residues are PLIP contacts in deposited structures, not wet-lab assay measurements; if used as any of those five, they inherit the Stage 0b family-straddle defect. See `gt_audit.md`. All 28 named atomic tools plus the unnamed eight-tool PubMed group are classified in `tool_readiness.csv`: 4 local backends installed, 18 installable, 7 absent; **no MCP server was built or certified**. AF3 weights require organization-specific noncommercial authorization and PyRosetta needs its institutional license; current CMU-host/architecture compatibility remains to be verified. Foundation tools lacking a classical analogue are flagged as a Test-C design gap, not a free pass.

**Method and stop:** CD-HIT 50% local identity with ≥80% shorter-sequence coverage, one CPU thread. Positive duplicate and negative nonhomolog controls, completeness assertions, structural same-entry exclusion, and W5 affinity controls are recorded in scripts/JSON beside the CSVs. Unknowns remain explicitly unknown, not passes. Stop here for panel band ruling; do not start Stage 2.
