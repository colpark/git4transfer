# W1/W2 MCP tool cards — construct validity (criterion e)

14 September 2026 UTC. These cards describe the **installed endpoints**, not aspirational versions. Stage 2's (a–d) receipts show execution, not agreement with ProteinGym `DMS_score` or MegaScale `ddG_ML`. A numeric accuracy below belongs to a *composite* unless explicitly attributed to the tool. `UNKNOWN` is not a criterion-(e) pass for a direct scorer. Retrieval, analysis and recording endpoints do not individually predict the measured endpoint, so their accuracy is not applicable; their contribution must be assessed through the frozen downstream decision metric. The accepted Stage 3 floors remain historical; this amendment does not rewrite them.

## Ground-truth cards and exposure

**W1 ProteinGym:** [Notin et al.](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/cac723e5ff29f65e3fcbb0739ae91bee-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html) aggregate heterogeneous DMS readouts—binding, expression, activity, growth—into per-assay `DMS_score`. Cross-assay raw scores are not comparable; the frozen decision is per-item P@10 then cluster-aware aggregation. 217 substitution assays/186 proteins is a release-level description, not our independent n (174 candidate clusters, 148 evaluated). Public pre-2025 material remains a memory-exposure risk despite Stage 3's 0/148 eight-gram and 0/20 recitation probes.

**W2 MegaScale:** [Tsuboyama et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06328-6) use cDNA-display proteolysis to estimate folding stability for 331 natural and 148 designed 40–72-aa domains. `ddG_ML` is assay-derived, not a single OpenMM energy. Stage 1 found 242 50%-identity clusters and 238 with a usable non-WT label; the source reports 199 clusters at 30% identity, emphasizing remaining dependence. Neither ThermoMPNN nor PROSTATA may be used as a supposedly zero-shot tool on these labels.

**Future scope:** W3/W4/W5 remain closed. Every added tool needs an equally specific card and endpoint comparison before reopening; the absence of a classical counterpart is **not** a defect by itself.
