**Finding: masked marginal, not wild-type marginal.** The live `benchmark-mcp/esm2_worker.py` at SHA-256 `4c6a2484805eb8484517355b528bc4894bfb53fe9ef80d85c347df17e8bde5f1` replaces token `position` with the tokenizer's mask token *before* the forward pass, then returns `log p(mutant | masked context) - log p(WT | masked context)`. `server.py` and `predictive.py` pass the original WT sequence and one-based position unchanged. The frozen W1 candidate set contains single substitutions, so one-mask scoring matches the [ProteinGym ESM masked-marginal implementation](https://github.com/OATML-Markslab/ProteinGym/blob/main/proteingym/baselines/esm/compute_fitness.py) on scheme; [Meier et al.](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/f51338d736f95dd42427296047067694-Abstract.html) define the distinction. ProteinGym's ESM-2 launch script selects `masked-marginals`; the general scoring script defaults to `wt-marginals`, so the launch setting matters.

**Discriminating positive and negative control.** For pinned `facebook/esm2_t6_8M_UR50D` revision `c731040fcd8d73dceaa04b0a8e6329b345b0f5df`, `ACDEFGHIKLMNPQRSTVWY`, A1R, direct inference gave masked-marginal `+0.1933317184`, matching the Stage 2 certified MCP receipt `call_id:e6042d79ef6448238dddb9d0f441b235` exactly. Deliberately omitting the mask gave the wild-type marginal `-2.8935132027`; this must-fail control would have exposed a scheme swap. No ESM-2 implementation change or cache invalidation is needed.

**What this does not establish.** Our server uses **8M** weights; released ProteinGym Floor M uses **650M** weights. Same scoring *scheme* does not mean same scores or same construct performance. The comparison must be labeled released-650M versus MCP-8M, not an identical-model agency comparison. Our worker accepts 1–1022 canonical amino acids, uses the supplied ProteinGym `target_seq` rather than a retrieved UniProt full-length sequence, no windowing, natural logs, and one-based positions. ESM-IF is likewise a locally computed score rather than proof of numerical equality to the released archive. The blind side-by-side remains a method/implementation sensitivity analysis until its P@10 and per-item correlations are measured.
