# Standalone behavioural observation: valid receipt, irrelevant evidence

This record concerns the **pre-Stage-3h** Qwen3-30B-A3B Q4 full-grant cell on frozen pilot item 2 (`../stage3g_2026-09-14/cell30_surface_unguided/`). It is not an accuracy result and no label was opened. The 30B chose `esm2_likelihood` in its first response, but supplied `mutant="G52L"`; the one-letter schema rejected it, so no usable FM score returned. Its first `submit_answer` was accepted. The accepted JSON selected ten variants and rejected five from 100 supplied. All ten selected picks cite the **same** resolving `blast_search` receipt, `call_id:7f4513b098374291a867ffe859581c51`, as both evidence and validation, and each has exactly one declared validation category. That BLAST receipt reports homology of the unchanged query; it has no per-variant score. The submission therefore has **zero variant-specific validation results**, despite zero fabricated receipt IDs. Two later duplicate submissions were rejected by the record server.

Trace anchors: `../stage3g_2026-09-14/cell30_surface_unguided/result.json` gives 9 classical attempts/1 usable value, 1 predictive attempt/0 usable, 5 record attempts/3 usable, 367.25 seconds, and falling response throughput. `../stage3g_2026-09-14/cell30_surface_unguided/submissions/pilot_item.json` contains the accepted ten-pick answer, repeated receipt and one-category validations. `../stage3g_2026-09-14/cell30_surface_unguided/trace.jsonl` preserves ordered calls and malformed FM arguments. The valid receipt can be mechanically resolved to its artifact, but does **not** substantiate a variant-level choice.

This is qualitatively the shallow-evaluation behaviour highlighted by [BioDesignBench v2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.06.723381v2), now observed in **one open-weight 30B diagnostic cell**. It does not establish a frequency, causal depth effect, or general replication of their multi-task result; those require the preregistered scored study. The narrow result stands on its own: tool selection and receipt validity did not guarantee evidence relevance or evaluation depth. New standing rule: **RECEIPT VALIDITY IS NOT EVIDENCE RELEVANCE.**
