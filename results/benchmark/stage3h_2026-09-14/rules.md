# Standing rules, amended 14 September 2026

- Receipt validity is not evidence relevance. A valid `call_id` proves a call occurred, not that it concerns the cited variant or supports the claimed inference.
- An asymmetric tool presentation is a confound, not a condition.
- A gate must protect against a named failure and must not duplicate hardware protection.
- A tool must measure the quantity the task scores on; a served checkpoint, including size and revision, is part of its identity.
- Meet the model's native encoding rather than forcing ours unless the encoding itself is under test.
- Not checked is not pass; controls must be capable of failing. An interrupted run is UNDEMONSTRATED, not a negative result.
- Do not widen a definition to make a result pass. Count the scarce unit. Report the decision metric, not the ranking metric alone. Unknowns stay unknown.

Stage 4 remains closed; these diagnostic cells are unscored and the eight original pilot items remain discarded.
