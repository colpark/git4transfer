# Stage 3h finding — native schema works; no clean full-item cell

14 September 2026 EST. **Stage 4 remains closed.** Four and only four authorized, unscored diagnostic cells ran on node 11 with agent/MCP on node 10. No assay labels were opened, no scorer or evaluator ran, and no additional pilot cells or Stage 4 arms were run. The eight original pilot items remain discarded.

**Schema and diagnostic item.** The seven variant-taking MCP tools/composites now accept `variant="G52L"` while preserving one-letter decomposed fields. JSON Schema rejects `mutant="G52L"` with the one-letter pattern; a conflicting pair fails with a message naming the disagreement. Positive and negative MCP controls passed, including real ESM-2 650M and ESM-IF1 variant calls and resolving receipts with `input_form`. The unchanged ≥80% BLAST/MSA coverage rule gave usable homologs for **131/140** non-pilot assays (9 unavailable), with median **79**; `ARGR_ECOLI_Tsuboyama_2023_1AOY` was selected at that median. This is a tool-coverage result, not a cohort or accuracy result.

**Equal-cap full-grant behavior.** Both Qwen 7B and Qwen3-30B-A3B ran at 4,096 output tokens per response; none hit the cap. 7B unguided made no tool call or submission. 7B guided made nine invalid BLAST attempts by passing variant strings as full protein sequences, then submitted two unsupported picks; the record server accepted the JSON but the frozen ten-pick contract failed, including a non-resolving citation. 30B selected ESM-2 in **both** modes and used `blast_msa` correctly, but omitted the variant in both ESM-2 calls, so **0/2 FM attempts produced a usable score**. In guided mode it later put `L27C` into the one-letter `mutant` field of a classical composite; schema rejection was correct. Neither subject used the new `variant` field under full task load. No submitted pick had variant-specific evidence. The isolated MCP controls show this is not server incapability; the four cells show the full-item argument-construction/relevance problem remains. The earlier Stage 3g 30B submission with one shared BLAST receipt and one validation category per pick is separately preserved in `depth_observation.md` as a one-cell behavioural observation, not a broad replication claim.

**Validity and disposition.** The session-level 60-second active-generation gate caught a 30B guided decline that the old per-response gate would miss: 54.9 versus 59.78 baseline-threshold tokens/s. 30B unguided also breached at 57.0 versus 62.27; both were terminated and are **UNDEMONSTRATED task outcomes**, not prediction failures. The 7B unguided timing passed after reconciling saved llama.cpp timings with a missed final live poll; guided 7B generated only 45.9 active seconds, so its session gate is UNDEMONSTRATED. GPU/CPU temperature, power, utilization and shared-memory headroom were reported, not used as safety aborts. No response cap was hit. Full per-run numbers and traces are in `cells_4x.md` and `cell_*/`.

**Recommendation to the panel:** do not open Stage 4 or infer an Arm 3 effect. A clean full-item cell—usable variant-specific FM value, contract-compliant submission, relevant receipts, and representative timing—has not been demonstrated. Rule on whether the median diagnostic assay must also be excluded from a future scored cohort, since it has now been used to learn about the harness; the prior eight are already excluded. Do not widen MSA coverage, coax the prompt, change scoring, or turn interrupted runs into zeros. The next authorization, if any, must re-register the cohort and tool surface explicitly.
